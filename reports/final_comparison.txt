======================================================================
KEY FINDINGS
======================================================================

1. Baseline vs RNN:
   Vanilla RNN failed completely due to vanishing gradient on
   long sequences (MAX_LEN=200). Accuracy stayed at ~50% (random).

2. RNN vs LSTM:
   LSTM's gates (input, forget, output) solved the vanishing
   gradient problem. Accuracy jumped from 50% to 85%.

3. LSTM vs Baseline:
   LSTM (85%) still underperforms Baseline (90%) without
   pretrained embeddings. TF-IDF captures global word statistics
   that LSTM needs more data/epochs to learn from scratch.

4. Baseline vs BERT:
   BERT (93%) outperforms Baseline (90%) by leveraging
   pretraining on 3.3B words. Understands context, negation,
   and sarcasm better than bag-of-words approaches.

5. Best model:
   BERT (DistilBERT) — 93.94% accuracy, 303 errors only.
   Error breakdown is balanced (159 FP, 144 FN) — no class bias.

======================================================================
RECOMMENDATION
======================================================================

   Use BERT for production — best accuracy and balanced errors.
   Use Baseline for fast inference — 90% accuracy with no GPU needed.
